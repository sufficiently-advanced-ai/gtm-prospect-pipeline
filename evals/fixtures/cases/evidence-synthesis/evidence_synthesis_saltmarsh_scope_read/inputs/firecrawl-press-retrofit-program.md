**Saltmarsh passes the halfway mark on its 22-vessel retrofit program** — 2026-01-30

Eleven tugs and barges now carry shore-power and scrubber retrofits. "Sequencing 22
vessels against dry-dock windows and charter commitments is a spreadsheet the size of a
wall," said Chief Innovation Officer Ingrid Halvorsen-Mbeki. "We re-plan it by hand every
time a yard slips." The program is due to complete in 2027.
