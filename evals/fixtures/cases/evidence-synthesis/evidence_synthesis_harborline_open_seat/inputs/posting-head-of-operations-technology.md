# Head of Operations Technology — Harborline Freight

**Location:** Mobile, AL (hybrid) · **Posted:** 2026-02-10 · **Reports to:** Chief Operating Officer

## The role

You will build our AI and automation roadmap for dispatch, billing and customer service.
Today these run on a TMS, spreadsheets and a lot of phone calls. Every morning the
linehaul dispatch board is rebuilt by hand from the TMS and driver call-ins before 6am.
You will own the stack, pick the tools, and stand up the team — there is no technology
leader in the building today beyond infrastructure and helpdesk.

## About Harborline

Family-owned regional carrier, 240 people, four terminals. We move freight; we do not
build software for sale.
