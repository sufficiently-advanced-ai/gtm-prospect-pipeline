**Harborline to open fifth terminal in Pascagoula in Q3 2026** — 2026-01-22

Harborline Freight announced a fifth terminal, adding 30 tractors and 22 drivers. "Five
terminals means five dispatch boards," said Chief Operating Officer Desmond Ferreira-Kole.
"Right now our dispatchers rebuild the linehaul board by hand every morning from the TMS
and the overnight driver calls. That does not scale to a fifth yard, and we are hiring a
technology leader to change it."
